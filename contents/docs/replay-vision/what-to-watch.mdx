---
title: What to watch
---

**Highlights** is the first tab on the Replay Vision home. Under the heading **Sessions worth watching**, it reads the [observations](/docs/replay-vision/observations) your scanners produced over a date range and picks the sessions that show signs of failures, struggles, or payment problems. You start with a short list of sessions instead of a table per scanner.

## What each card shows

Each session is a card. The cards fill one to four columns, depending on how wide the window is. Each card shows:

- **Scanner** – the scanner's name, with an icon in its type's color. Hover it to see the scanner's question and verdict. Click it to open the scanner.
- **Person and time** – who was recorded, with a link to their person profile, and when the scan ran.
- **Headline** – the summary's title for summarizers. For other scanner types, it's the first sentence of the scan's reasoning.
- **Why this recording** – one line on why the session made the feed. Hover it to read the full line.
- **Thumbnail** – a frame from the recording at the key moment. The key moment is where the scan's answer rests most.
- **Watch from** – starts the recording at the key moment. Recordings without a key moment show **Watch session** instead.

Cards for observations you haven't opened yet are marked **New**. This is tracked per user, and opening the observation's page clears it.

To hide a card you don't need, click **Not useful** (the **×** on the card) and pick a reason, or **Hide without a reason**. The feed stops showing that observation to you.

## Watching a session

Click a card to open its [observation page](/docs/replay-vision/observations#the-observation-page). The player there starts 3 seconds before the key moment, so you see what led up to it. Recordings without a key moment start from the beginning. Going back returns you to the feed.

## Why a session is in the feed

Every card has a **Why this recording** line that names the kind of moment the session shows:

| Reason                                    | What it means                                                   |
| ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Something failed in this session          | Errors, failed requests, clicks that did nothing, or dead ends  |
| The user struggled in this session        | Repeated attempts, rage clicks, abandoned forms, or workarounds |
| A payment problem came up in this session | Failed payments, cancellations and downgrades, or usage limits  |
| New since you last looked                 | Nothing stood out, and you haven't opened it yet                |
| The newest from this scanner              | Nothing stood out, and it's among the most recent               |

The last two only pad out a feed that has few findings. If every card is one of them, the feed says that nothing stood out in this window and shows the newest sessions.

## Similar sessions

The feed shows one card for a group of sessions that look alike. When a card stands for more sessions than its own, the reason line counts them, like "Something failed here and in 3 similar sessions."

Click the **similar sessions** button on the card to open the list in a popover. The popover shows how many people the sessions came from. If the group has more sessions than the popover shows, click **See all similar sessions** to open the full list on the scanner's **Observations** tab.

The count and the list include only the kinds of moment that you show in the feed.

## Filtering the feed

The controls at the top of the feed narrow what it picks from:

- **All scanners** or **My scanners** – show sessions from every scanner, or only from the scanners you created. This menu shows only if you created a scanner.
- **Date range** – the last 7 days by default. You can pick the last 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days, or 90 days.
- **Customize feed** (gear icon) – pick the kinds of moment to include.

### Choosing the kinds of moment

Click the gear icon and select or clear the kinds of moment:

- **Failures** – errors, failed requests, clicks that did nothing, dead ends.
- **Struggles** – repeated attempts, rage clicks, abandoned forms, workarounds.
- **Payment or checkout problems** – failed payments, cancellations and downgrades, usage limits.

All three are on by default. You must keep at least one on. When you hide a kind, its sessions leave the feed, and a dot on the gear icon shows that some kinds are hidden. The other sessions keep their rank, so hiding a kind doesn't bring weaker sessions into the feed.

Your choice is saved in your browser for each project. A kind that PostHog adds later shows by default.

## When the feed is empty

An empty feed tells you why, and what to do about it:

- **No scanners yet** – the tab explains the feed with example cards and offers to create your first scanner.
- **Your Replay Vision credits are used up for this period** – scans are skipped until the period resets or you raise the limit. See [quota and limits](/docs/replay-vision/quota-and-limits).
- **All scanners on this project are off** – turn one on and its sessions appear after the next matching recording.
- **Every running scanner has reached its credit limit for this period** – they stop scanning until the period resets. Raise a scanner's limit to start it again. Sessions they skipped aren't scanned later.
- **No sessions from your scanners stood out** – click **Show all scanners** to see sessions from scanners that other people created.
- **No sessions stood out** – click the button to try a longer date range.

## Using the API or MCP

The feed is also available through the `highlight_feed` endpoint and the `vision-scanners-highlight-feed` [MCP tool](/docs/replay-vision/mcp). Pass `categories` as a comma-separated list of `failure`, `struggle`, and `revenue` to include only those kinds of moment. Leave it out to include all three.

To list the similar sessions behind a card, filter a scanner's observations with `watch_group` set to the card's observation ID. Add `watch_category` with the same kinds that you passed as `categories`, so the list matches the card's count.
